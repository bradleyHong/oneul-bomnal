Media art made with code, not a timeline

Most signage vendors deliver one mp4. If the next lobby is 1:6 instead of 16:9, that file is wrong. We write a deterministic piece: the same seed on the same canvas always draws the same picture, then we print it at the site ratio. Public APIs or an institution’s database can drive color, density, or motion when a live picture is wanted. If you only need a beautiful loop, we still deliver 4K video that drops onto the player you already own.

  • Generative and AI-assisted media art
  • Re-output to 32:9, 16:9, 9:16, 1:6, L-shaped, or a mapped wall
  • Three concepts within a week (outsourced video often takes a month)
  • Live weather, air quality, or in-house data when useful

LED panels and outdoor displays

We size, supply, and install lobby media walls, building-face LED, and vertical signage — including structure, cabling, and power. If a panel is already on the wall, we match its resolution and input and change only the content.

Projector media facades

We survey the wall, choose projector count and brightness, map around windows and columns, and run dusk-timed playback. Festival and opening-week installs include take-down. Daytime or short-throw sites that cannot hold a projector are usually better as LED — we say so after the survey.

FAQ

Service questions

Answers written so they stand on their own.

What is a media facade?

A media facade uses a building exterior or gallery wall as one large image. 오늘은 봄날 typically does this with projectors and projection mapping: the picture is warped to windows, columns, and cornices instead of hanging a new screen. Night festivals, openings, and indoor monumental walls are the usual fit.

Do I need a new LED if I already have a panel?

No. Existing lobby walls, outdoor boards, and information totems can keep their hardware. The studio matches resolution and input and supplies new media-art content. A survey still checks viewing distance, brightness, and power before the spec is locked.

Can institutional data appear on the screen?

Yes, when the values are numeric or scheduled — visitors, energy, bookings, air quality, notices. Public APIs can be read live; in-house databases are connected through a relay that follows the institution’s security rules. The result is color, particles, and motion, not a spreadsheet on the wall.

What does content cost?

Published prices, VAT excluded: one work KRW 1,000,000; four seasonal works KRW 3,900,000; ten custom works in a year KRW 9,000,000. Installation of LED or projectors is not in those figures and is quoted after a site survey.
