How it works

Four steps, no account

  1. Pick a screenThirty-odd subjects — a bamboo grove, a moon jar, an inlaid celadon, a golden Buddha wall, a reaction-diffusion field. Press again and the same subject comes back different. Nothing is a stock loop.
  2. Set the panelEnter the real pixel dimensions and the loop length. The work is redrawn at that ratio rather than stretched to fit.
  3. Hang it on a wallYour choice is mapped onto photographs of real signage — a city hall board, a lobby column, a two-sided column, a building frontage — at true scale, so you can see how large it actually reads.
  4. Approve and receiveThe final render arrives within 24 business hours on business days. Every work carries a number; the same number always redraws the same screen.

A note on language. ArtWork Studio's own interface is currently in Korean. The controls are largely visual — you press a tile, you get a screen — and every work carries a number you can quote back to us in English. If you would rather not use the tool at all, describe what you want on the contact page and we will send concepts instead.

Everything is drawn by our code. No photographs or video are composited in. Works that show three-dimensional form take vertex coordinates from public-domain (CC0) models and redraw them as lines and points in our own code — no one else's image is ever output. Samples shown in the tool are not stored and may not be used commercially.
