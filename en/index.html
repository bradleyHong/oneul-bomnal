Who we are

오늘은 봄날 is a women-owned media-art AX studio based in Suseong-gu, Daegu, Republic of Korea. The public site is publicbloom.art. We do not edit a finished video and walk away: we write the artwork in code so the same piece can be re-rendered to a 32:9 outdoor LED, a 9:16 lobby totem, or a mapped building wall. When a live feed is useful, weather, air quality, or an institution’s own database can change the picture every day. When the site has no screen yet, we install LED panels or a projector media facade ourselves.

What is 오늘은 봄날 (Oneul-eun Bomnal)?

오늘은 봄날 is a women-owned media-art studio in Suseong-gu, Daegu, Korea. The website is publicbloom.art. The studio writes media art in code, produces it through an AI-assisted pipeline, and can install the LED display or projector media facade that shows the work. Representative: Park Seongsil. Business registration 575-19-00976.

What does “media art made with code” mean?

The picture is generated by a program, not cut on a video timeline. The same approved piece can be output again at another aspect ratio and resolution. If public data or an institution’s database is connected, the same screen can look different every day. If you only need a file, the studio still delivers 4K video sized to the panel.

Can a foreign company or museum commission work?

Yes. Content can be specified, approved, and delivered remotely as 4K video or live code. Write to studio@publicbloom.art in English, Japanese, French, or German. LED or projector installation in Korea is quoted after a site survey. Work outside the listed Korean regions is arranged case by case.

Why does women-owned status matter for Korean public buyers?

오늘은 봄날 holds a women-owned business certificate (No. 0113-2025-35456) and is therefore eligible for public-sector priority purchasing in Korea. It also holds a direct-production certificate for video production (No. 2025-0495-02141), which allows immediate participation in restricted SME tenders.

How fast is a first concept?

Three concepts are returned within one week of a brief. Conventional outsourced video often takes a month or more. After approval, a weekday render is delivered within 24 hours (weekends and Korean public holidays excluded).

Media facade or LED — which is it?

A media facade here means projectors mapped onto an existing wall, usually at night. An LED display is a physical screen fixed to the building and can run in daylight. 오늘은 봄날 builds both and recommends after measuring the wall, ambient light, throw distance, and power.
