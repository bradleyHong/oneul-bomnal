What to send

Four lines are enough

  1. The wallWidth and height in metres, or the panel's pixel dimensions if you already know them. A photograph taken from where people will stand is worth more than a measurement.
  2. The siteIndoors or out, and how bright it gets. Daylight and darkness need different work.
  3. The dateWhen it has to be lit. If it is an event rather than a permanent installation, say so — a rental may cost less than an installation.
  4. Who you areThe institution and a contact. For Korean public bodies, tell us the fiscal year and we will format the quotation for it.
