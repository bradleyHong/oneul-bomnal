FAQ

Before you book

What does it cost?

₩5,000,000 for a base rental of three days. That includes media art made for your institution and both installation and strike, so there is no separate content commission. We install the day before the event. Each further day is ₩1,000,000 — the content already exists by then, so only the projector and equipment are charged. That is why the per-day cost falls on multi-day festivals.

Why is three days the minimum?

Because we install the day before. Arriving on the morning of the event leaves no time to set focus and brightness against the actual ambient light. Hanging it the day before and switching it on once after dark is what makes it look right on the day. Even a single-day event is booked as three, covering the install day and the strike.

We have no footage to play. Can we still book?

Yes, and the content is included in the base price. Tell us about the institution and we make the media art for it. If you already hold footage, we re-render it to the screen's dimensions instead. Because we build in code rather than editing video, the same work comes back out at whatever ratio and resolution the site needs.

Does someone have to be on site?

No. The player starts on power-up and keeps running. If playback stops it notices and restarts itself. No keyboard and no trained operator are needed at the venue.

Will it work outdoors with no wall?

That is what it is for. It projects onto tents, booths and bare walls. Being short-throw, it produces a 3m image in spaces with no room to stand back. It can hang from a frame or sit on the ground.

Where do you travel?

Daegu and Gyeongbuk first, then Busan, Gyeongnam and Ulsan — we install and strike in person. Beyond that we arrange it around the travel schedule.
