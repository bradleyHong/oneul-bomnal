FAQ

Before you write

Practical notes for buyers outside Korea.

Do you accept work from outside Korea?

Yes. Remote content commissions are routine. Describe the city, the screen, and the deadline. Installation travel outside Daegu–Busan–Gyeongbuk–Gyeongnam–Ulsan is discussed per project.

What should the first email include?

Organization name, a reachable email, wall or panel size (or a photo), and whether you need content only, hardware, or both. Budget range is optional. The more specific the brief, the tighter the first concepts.

How are public-sector documents handled?

For Korean government and foundation buyers, the studio issues fiscal-year quotes plus the women-owned and direct-production certificates on request. Foreign institutional purchase orders are handled by email.

Overseas clients are welcome. Write in English, Japanese, French, or German — we reply by email. Installation in Korea is scheduled after a site survey; content can be delivered remotely as 4K video or live code.
